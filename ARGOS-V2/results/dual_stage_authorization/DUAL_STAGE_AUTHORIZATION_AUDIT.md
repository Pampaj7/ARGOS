# ARGOS v2 — Dual-stage authorization audit pointer

The pre-implementation scientific audit is canonical at
`model_design/DUAL_STAGE_AUTHORIZATION_AUDIT.md`. It records the frozen modules,
conditional population, predeclared C0-C3 ladder, threshold grids, leakage
policy, selection rule, and promotion criteria before any final-test result was
opened.

C3 was not justified. Final keyframes 3/4 failed the strict promotion gate, so
unseen backbones and OOD datasets remained unopened.
